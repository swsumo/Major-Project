🔐 How Federated Learning Works

Understanding the privacy-preserving AI technology behind FitAI

🧠 FL Architecture

📥

Step 1: Receive

Each client receives the global model from the server

🎯

Step 2: Train Locally

Clients train on their private data. Data never leaves the device!

📤

Step 3: Aggregate

Only model updates are sent back. Server averages them (FedAvg)

❌ Traditional ML (Centralized)

📤
Upload Raw Data

Your weight, health metrics, meal logs uploaded to server

🗄️
Centralized Storage

All user data stored in one place (privacy risk!)

⚠️
Privacy Concerns

Data breaches, unauthorized access, third-party sharing

📊 Data Uploaded: 500 MB

✅ Federated Learning (FitAI)

🔒
Data Stays Local

Your data NEVER leaves your device. Completely private!

🧠
Learn Patterns Only

AI learns from patterns across users, not individual data

🛡️
Maximum Privacy

No data breaches possible. Your health info is yours!

📊 Data Uploaded: 2 MB (model weights only)
